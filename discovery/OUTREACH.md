# Outreach copy — asking customizers for before/after pairs

**Where:** r/DIY, r/femalefashionadvice (DIY thread), r/malefashionadvice, r/rawdenim, r/sewing, r/ThriftStoreHauls, r/upcycling;
sewing/thrift Discords; Facebook upcycling groups. One post per community, no cross-posting spam; reply to every comment.

**Title:** Cutting jeans into shorts? I'm building an open dataset to predict how the frayed hem will turn out — 2-minute photo contribution

**Body:**
I'm a student researcher building an open, CC-BY dataset of jeans **before and after being cut into shorts** (and after the
first wash), to train a model that predicts how *your specific pair* will look — cut placement, how much it frays, how the
hem sits. Existing AI image tools invent a different garment; this one is meant to keep every pocket, fade and stain.

If you're about to cut a pair (or already did), here's what helps:
1. Jeans laid flat on a plain floor/sheet, photographed from directly above, whole garment in frame.
2. **A coin or ruler on the floor next to the hem** — that's how we get real-world size.
3. The same shot after cutting, and after the first wash. A close-up of the frayed hem is gold.
4. The care label, and how you washed/dried it.

Submit here (GitHub form, photos attach directly, credit or anonymous): https://github.com/jefferyjefe/denim-twin/issues/new?template=pair-submission.yml
Everything is open: code, method notes and honest results (including what doesn't work yet) at https://github.com/jefferyjefe/denim-twin.

**Rules of engagement:** never DM people for photos; only photos they took; thank and credit; post results back to the thread.
