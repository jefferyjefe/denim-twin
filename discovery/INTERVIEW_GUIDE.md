# Customer Discovery — Interview Guide (plan §15)

Run in parallel with research. Focus on **past behavior**, not opinions about the idea.
Do not describe the product until the last section.

## Targets (log in `discovery/INTERVIEWS.md`)
| Segment | Target n | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Casual thrift customizers | 10 | 0 |
| Frequent upcyclers / resale sellers | 10 | 0 |
| Professional denim / apparel designers | 5 | 0 |
| Alteration / repair / customization businesses | 5 | 0 |

## Script (~25 min)

**Warm-up (2 min)** — how often they modify clothing; what kinds.

**Last modification (8 min)**
1. Tell me about the last garment you modified. Walk me through it start to finish.
2. What decision felt most risky? Why?
3. How did you plan or preview the result before you started? (Sketch? Pin it? Try on? Nothing?)
4. What did you measure, if anything?

**Failures (5 min)**
5. Have you ruined a garment? What happened, and what did it cost you (money, time, sentiment)?
6. Is there a garment you've wanted to modify but haven't? What's stopping you?

**Patterns (5 min)**
7. Which modifications do you repeat most often? (Listen for: cut-offs, hems, cropping, distressing, dye, patches.)
8. What do you currently spend on samples, mistakes, or design labor per month/year?

**Preview reaction — only now describe the concept (5 min)**
9. If you could see a realistic preview of *your exact garment* after a cut, would that be enough, or would you also need measurements / instructions?
10. Would you take several photos or a short video to get it? What's the most you'd do?
11. What would make you trust the preview? What would make you distrust it?
12. How often would you realistically use something like this?

## Red flags
- Only "that sounds cool" with no matching past behavior → weak evidence.
- Wants the tool for garments they don't own yet (shopping preview) → different product.
