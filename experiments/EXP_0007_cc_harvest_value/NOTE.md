# EXP_0007 — Is the CC-licensed harvest (Openverse + Commons) useful? (negative)

Manifest: 788 records (Openverse 217 new after the page_size fix, Commons 274). Curator (CLIP zero-shot) over the first
40 downloads: flat_lay 7, on_model 24, detail 9, junk 3. Contact sheet (harvest_sheet.jpg) of the 16 flat_lay/detail
images: a denim coat on a mannequin, a warehouse rack, fabric macros, three photos of an **insect on sand**, paper
cups with a denim print, pocket/label close-ups. Zero flat-laid jeans or cut-off shorts.

## Read
- The free-image pool has almost no flat-laid whole jeans; what it has is macros and product shots.
- The CLIP zero-shot curator mislabels aggressively (insects → flat_lay); it is not a usable filter without a few
  hundred labelled examples.
- Value of this stream for the project: fabric-texture macros (maybe useful for weave/texture priors later); nothing
  for cut/fringe prediction. Curator throttled to daily; keep the manifest, stop expecting garments from it.
